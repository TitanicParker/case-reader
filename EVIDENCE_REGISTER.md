# Evidence Register

This register is a public-facing inventory of the document classes referenced by the case reader. It is designed to separate the interpretive narrative from the underlying evidentiary record.

## Evidence-status labels

- **Primary record** — original clinical, statutory, or institutional document.
- **Correspondence** — letters, emails, complaint materials, regulatory exchanges, or response records.
- **Interpretive companion** — explanatory material prepared to assist reading of the primary record.
- **Pending upload** — document not yet included in the repository.
- **Redaction required** — document should not be published until identifying, clinical, contact, and third-party information has been reviewed.

## Publication cautions

Before any primary record is placed in a public repository, review it for:

- patient identifiers;
- hospital numbers;
- clinician names, unless publication is legally and strategically intended;
- addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, PPS/NHS or insurance identifiers;
- third-party names;
- signatures and barcodes;
- prescription or pharmacy identifiers;
- metadata embedded in PDFs or images.

The site can remain public-facing without publishing raw records immediately. The safer first version is a structured reading aid with document placeholders and clear status labels.

## Editorial rule

Every interpretive claim should be traceable to one of three sources:

1. the primary record;
2. the chronology created by the order of records;
3. a clearly marked interpretive inference.

This rule keeps the site restrained, forensic, and reviewable.
